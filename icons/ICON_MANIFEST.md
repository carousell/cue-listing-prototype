## Known export issues

- 13 `system/` icons have off-by-one viewBoxes from Figma bounding-box rounding
  (e.g. `action/32_camera.svg` is `33x32`).
- `Social/15_Group.svg` exported at 15px; imported as `system/social/16_group.svg`.
- `Listing/32_Bump-1.svg` was byte-identical to `32_Bump.svg` and was dropped.
- Dropped as duplicates: `action/add_tag_1` (the same glyph flattened to one
  merged path), `property/clock` (identical to `listing/clock`), and
  `action/search_1` (identical to `action/search` at 16 and 32px, and a
  noticeably heavier stroke at 24px).
- 97 `object/` icons shipped with a full-bleed `#EBEBEB` Figma frame background,
  and two also carried Figma's dashed section marker. Both were stripped.
